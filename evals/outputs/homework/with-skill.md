# Year 8 French homework: Perfect tense with avoir

**Assumptions (change any you like):** about 20 minutes, set for a week, no AI (it's all retrieval and short writing, so AI would skip the learning), nobody needs to speak French at home, and you'll check it with a 5-minute swap-and-mark next lesson. I've kept to regular -er verbs plus *fait* and *vu*, since you've only just finished avoir.

## STUDENT SHEET (print or paste into your platform)

# French homework: What did you do?

**Time:** 20 minutes. **Use:** your brain only, no AI, no notes, no dictionary.
**Rule:** do Parts 1 to 3 from memory first. Only then turn to the answer box at the bottom and check in a different colour pen.

### Part 1: Pick the right one (5 min)
Circle one letter.

1. Which means "I played football"?
   A. Je joue au foot  B. Je suis joué au foot  C. J'ai joué au foot  D. J'ai jouer au foot
2. What is the past participle of *manger*?
   A. mangé  B. mangeé  C. mangi  D. mangez
3. "Nous avons regardé un film" means:
   A. We watch a film  B. I watched a film  C. We will watch a film  D. We watched a film
4. Fill the gap: Elle ___ mangé une pizza.
   A. est  B. a  C. ai  D. ont
5. How do you say "they listened to music"?
   A. Ils ai écouté de la musique  B. Ils a écouté de la musique  C. Ils écouté de la musique  D. Ils ont écouté de la musique
6. How do you say "my brother"?
   A. mon frère  B. ma frère  C. mes frère  D. mon sœur
7. "I hate sport because it's boring" is:
   A. J'aime le sport parce que c'est ennuyeux  B. Je déteste le sport parce que c'est génial  C. Je déteste le sport parce que c'est ennuyeux  D. J'adore le sport parce que c'est nul
8. Which sentence is in the past?
   A. Je regarde la télé  B. J'ai regardé la télé  C. Je vais regarder la télé  D. Je veux regarder la télé

### Part 2: Translate into French (6 min)
Write full sentences.
1. I played with my sister.
2. My dad watched a film.
3. We ate pizza.
4. I hate homework because it's boring.

### Part 3: Your weekend (7 min)
Write **5 sentences** in French about what you or your family did last weekend. Use the frame if you need it:

> Le week-end dernier, j'ai ______ (verb) avec mon / ma / mes ______ (family member).
> Mon frère / ma mère / mon père ______ (a / as) ______ (verb).
> Nous avons ______.
> J'ai aimé / J'ai détesté ______ parce que c'est ______.

Challenge (optional): add one sentence with *j'ai fait* or *j'ai vu*.

### Part 4: Say it out loud (2 min, at home)
Read your Part 3 sentences out loud to someone at home. **That person doesn't need to know any French.** Their only job: listen, then ask "Did you say what you meant to say?" and sign below. You can also read to a pet, a mirror or your phone's voice recorder.

Signed: ______________

### Check your work (do this last)
Part 1: C, A, D, B, D, A, C, B
Part 2: 1. J'ai joué avec ma sœur. 2. Mon père a regardé un film. 3. Nous avons mangé une pizza. 4. Je déteste les devoirs parce que c'est ennuyeux.
Mark any you got wrong in a different colour and write the correct version. Part 3: check every verb has *a / ai / as / avons / ont* plus an -é ending.

## TEACHER NOTES

**Why this design**
- Roughly 70% retrieval of avoir + past participles, with spaced items from earlier in the term (family: Q6, Part 2 Qs 1 and 2; opinions: Q7, Part 2 Q4, Part 3 last line). Part 3 stretches the new tense into a short extended answer.
- Nothing needs teaching at home. The parent role is tiny and clear: **the student reads aloud, the adult only listens and signs.** No French needed, and it gives students a reason to rehearse speaking.
- Students attempt from memory before the answer box, so checking is learning, not copying.

**MCQ answer key (checked, no patterns):** C A D B D A C B. Two of each letter, no repeats, no runs, no staircases, no alternation, no repeating block. Checked with the bundled key checker: "clean". If you reorder options, re-check.

**Mark scheme (total 28)**
- Part 1: 1 mark each (8).
- Part 2: 2 marks each (8): 1 for correct auxiliary (a/ai/avons), 1 for correct participle and spelling. Accept *mon père a regardé un film* and *j'ai joué avec ma sœur*. Accept "c'est" or "ils sont" variants only if sensible.
- Part 3: 10 marks. 5 sentences x 2 marks: 1 for correct avoir form, 1 for correct participle. Bonus 1 for correctly used opinion + *parce que*; bonus 1 for fait/vu. Ignore accent slips for the first mark.
- Common errors to look for: using *être* (*je suis joué*), missing the avoir verb, infinitive instead of participle (*j'ai jouer*), *ma frère*.

**Checking and next lesson (5 min):** swap books, mark Part 1 and 2 from the board, then pick two students to read a Part 3 sentence aloud. Collect Part 3 for a quick tick-and-comment glance only. Use the three most common errors as tomorrow's starter retrieval questions.

**Sending to parents (optional short message):** "This week's French homework needs no French from you. Please just listen while your child reads their sentences aloud for two minutes, ask 'Did you say what you meant to say?' and sign the sheet. Thank you!"
