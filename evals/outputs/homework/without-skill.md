# Year 8 French: Weekly Homework (Perfect Tense with avoir)

**Assumptions:** The class has met regular -er, -ir and -re participles plus a few common irregulars (fait, eu, pris). They have not yet done the negative of the perfect tense or être verbs, so neither appears. Total time is about 30 to 35 minutes, split over three short sittings so it works around busy evenings. Everything below the line "Teacher pack" is for you; everything above it can be copied straight onto a sheet or your VLE.

## Note for parents and carers (you do not need any French!)

Your child is practising talking about things that **happened in the past**, using family words and opinions. You do not need to understand a word of French to help. Here is your job:

1. **Be the audience.** Ask your child to read their French sentences aloud to you, then explain what each one means in English. If they can explain it clearly, they understand it.
2. **Use the answer sheet.** Your child brings home a sheet in the box at the bottom of Task 1 and 2 that has the answers. Please do not tell them the answers early. Once they have finished, you can tick or circle anything that does not match and let them correct it in a different colour.
3. **Use the Pattern Box below.** In Task 4, check they have used the pattern "**J'ai** + word ending in -é" at least three times. You can check this by looking, not by knowing French.
4. **Sign at the end.** A quick signature tells me it was done and discussed.

If it gets stressful, stop and write "stuck" next to the question. I would rather see an honest "stuck" than a copied answer.

## Pattern Box (keep this open while you work)

**Perfect tense = a helper (avoir) + a past participle**

| Person | Helper |
|---|---|
| j' | **ai** |
| tu | **as** |
| il / elle | **a** |
| nous | **avons** |
| vous | **avez** |
| ils / elles | **ont** |

**Making the past participle**

| Verb ending | Change to | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -er | -é | jouer → joué |
| -ir | -i | finir → fini |
| -re | -u | vendre → vendu |

**Irregular ones to learn:** faire → **fait**, avoir → **eu**, prendre → **pris**

**Family words you can use:** ma mère, mon père, mon frère, ma sœur, mes parents, mes grands-parents, mon oncle, ma tante, mon cousin, ma cousine

**Opinions:** j'aime, j'adore, je déteste, je n'aime pas, c'est génial, c'est nul, parce que c'était... (because it was...)

## Sitting 3 (about 10 to 15 minutes): Task 4

### Task 4: My last weekend (writing)
Write **5 to 6 sentences in French** about something you did with your family at the weekend (or a day out, a meal, a birthday, anything real or imagined).

**You must include:**
- at least **3 sentences** with the perfect tense (J'ai... / Mon père a... / Nous avons...)
- at least **2 different family members**

# TEACHER PACK

## Why it is built this way
- **No parent French needed.** The parent's role is audience, checker and signer: they listen to the child explain in English, compare against an answer strip, and look for visible patterns. This also gives the child a retrieval and explanation task, which is stronger practice than silent writing.
- **Spaced and cumulative.** Task 4 pulls in family vocabulary and opinions from earlier in the term alongside the new perfect tense, so nothing is learned in isolation.
- **Graded difficulty.** Tasks 1 and 2 are recall and accuracy; Task 3 is controlled translation; Task 4 is open writing with a starter frame for weaker writers and the "include" list as a stretch for stronger ones.
- **Short sittings.** Three chunks of 10 to 15 minutes are easier to fit around busy households than one 35 minute block.

## Answers

**Task 1:** joué, mangé, regardé, fini, attendu, fait, eu, pris

**Task 2:** 1. ai 2. avons 3. a 4. ont 5. avez 6. as

**Task 3:**
1. J'ai joué avec mon frère.
2. Ma mère a regardé la télé.
3. Nous avons mangé avec mes grands-parents.
4. Mes cousins ont acheté un cadeau.

(Accept "la télévision" or "regardé la télé". Do not penalise "J'ai joué avec mon frère" if the child writes "mon frere" without the accent in Task 3 since accents are assessed separately in Task 4.)

**Task 4 model answer (for reference, not to be shared as a copy template):**
> Le weekend dernier, j'ai mangé au restaurant avec mes grands-parents. Mon père a pris une pizza et ma sœur a mangé un burger. Nous avons regardé un film après. J'ai adoré ça parce que c'était génial. Je déteste les films tristes parce que c'est nul.

## Mark scheme (total 30)

| Task | Marks | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8 | 1 per correct participle |
| 2 | 6 | 1 per correct form of avoir |
| 3 | 8 | 2 per sentence: 1 for correct avoir form, 1 for correct participle and vocabulary |
| 4 | 8 | see below |

**Task 4 (8 marks):**
- 3 marks: perfect tense sentences (1 per correct sentence, up to 3)
- 2 marks: two family members used correctly
- 2 marks: two opinions with a reason (1 each)
- 1 mark: overall accuracy and effort (spelling, gender agreement with mon/ma, accents)

Award 1 bonus "merit" point for any correct irregular participle (fait, eu, pris) used in Task 4, or an extra tense/detail beyond the brief.

## Common errors to watch for
- Using **être** instead of **avoir** (J'es mangé / Je suis mangé)
- Forgetting the participle ending (J'ai mange instead of J'ai mangé)
- Mixing up **mon / ma / mes** with the family member
- Writing the infinitive instead of the participle (J'ai jouer)
- Dropping the apostrophe (Jai)

## Follow-up for next lesson (5 minutes)
- Start the lesson with a quick-fire "Who has the best weekend sentence?" volunteers read aloud, class translates.
- Have pairs swap sheets and check Task 4 against the checklist in two minutes. This also catches any homework that was clearly adult-written, since children can usually explain their own sentences.

## Accessibility and fairness notes
- Students with limited home support can complete Tasks 1 to 3 in a lunchtime or homework club session; offer this explicitly.
- If a household has a different home language, the child can explain sentences in that language instead of English. The point is that they can articulate meaning.
- For students who find writing hard, accept Task 4 at 4 sentences if all required elements are included.
