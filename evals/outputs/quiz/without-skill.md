# Year 7 Geography: Rivers Check-In Quiz

*Low-stakes, 12 questions, 4 options each. Circle one answer per question.*

**Assumptions:** the unit covered the upper, middle and lower course, the main erosion and transport processes, and flooding. Because it finished two weeks ago, the questions are recall and basic understanding, with no map or grid-reference skills required.

---

**1. What is the place where a river begins called?**
- A. The mouth
- B. The meander
- C. The source
- D. The estuary

**2. Which list shows a river's course in order from start to finish?**
- A. Upper course, middle course, lower course
- B. Lower course, middle course, upper course
- C. Middle course, upper course, lower course
- D. Upper course, lower course, middle course

**3. What is a small river that joins a larger river called?**
- A. An estuary
- B. A floodplain
- C. A watershed
- D. A tributary

**4. What do we call the wearing away of a river's bed and banks?**
- A. Deposition
- B. Erosion
- C. Transportation
- D. Evaporation

**5. Which type of erosion happens when rocks carried by the river scrape and scratch the bed and banks?**
- A. Solution
- B. Hydraulic action
- C. Attrition
- D. Abrasion

**6. How does a river move large, heavy stones along its bed?**
- A. By rolling them along (traction)
- B. By carrying them high in the water (suspension)
- C. By dissolving them completely
- D. By freezing them into the banks

**7. Why does a river deposit (drop) the material it is carrying?**
- A. It speeds up and gains energy
- B. It flows down a steeper slope
- C. It slows down and loses energy
- D. It has no water left in it

**8. How does a waterfall form?**
- A. A river flows into the sea and the water drops
- B. A river flows over hard rock on top of softer rock, which erodes faster and leaves a step
- C. A river floods and the water spills over its banks
- D. A river deposits sand in a pile at its mouth

**9. What is a meander?**
- A. A bend or curve in a river, common in the middle and lower course
- B. A very steep, narrow valley near the source
- C. A pool of water at the bottom of a waterfall
- D. A flat area where a river meets the sea

**10. What happens on the outside bend of a meander?**
- A. The river drops sand and pebbles, forming a beach
- B. The water is very slow and shallow
- C. Nothing changes, as the water moves evenly across the river
- D. The water flows fastest and erodes the bank, forming a river cliff

**11. What is the flat, low-lying land on either side of a river in its lower course, which can be covered in water when the river floods?**
- A. A plateau
- B. A floodplain
- C. A watershed
- D. A gorge

**12. Which of these makes a river flood more likely?**
- A. Planting lots of trees along the river
- B. A long spell of dry, sunny weather
- C. Heavy rain falling on ground that is already soaked
- D. Building a wide, empty reservoir upstream

---

ANSWER KEY: C A D B D A C B A D B C
