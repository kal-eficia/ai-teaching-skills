## PAGE 1: THE ORGANISER

### 1. The four claimants (January 1066)
Edward the Confessor died in January 1066 with no children.

| Claimant | Who | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Harold Godwinson** | Earl of Wessex, the richest English noble | Chosen by the Witan; crowned 6 January |
| **William** | Duke of Normandy | Edward promised him the throne; Harold swore an oath to support him (1064) |
| **Harald Hardrada** | King of Norway | Claimed the throne through an earlier deal between Norwegian and English kings |
| **Edgar the Ætheling** | Edward's teenage great-nephew | Royal blood, but too young and with little support |

### 2. The battles
- 2.1 **Stamford Bridge** (near York), **25 Sept 1066**: Harold beat Hardrada and his own brother Tostig.
- 2.2 Three days later William landed at **Pevensey** in the south.
- 2.3 Harold's tired army marched about 250 miles south to meet him.
- 2.4 **Hastings**, **14 Oct 1066**: the English **shield wall** held on the hill until some soldiers broke ranks and chased the Normans.
- 2.5 William's **archers and cavalry** and a **feigned retreat** won the battle. Harold was killed.
- 2.6 William was crowned king on **Christmas Day 1066**.

### 3. How William kept control
- 3.1 **Castles**: built across England. First **motte and bailey** (timber, quick to build), later stone.
- 3.2 **Feudal system**: the king gave land to barons in return for loyalty and soldiers, and so on down the pyramid.
- 3.3 **Domesday Book (1086)**: a survey of who owned what, used to tax people fairly (for the king).

```
        KING (William)
         BARONS
         KNIGHTS
   PEASANTS (worked the land)
```

### 4. Key words
**Claimant**: person who says they have the right to be king. **Witan**: council of English nobles who advised the king. **Heir**: person who inherits. **Earl**: powerful English noble. **Shield wall**: soldiers locked shields in a tight line. **Feigned retreat**: pretending to run away to trick the enemy. **Motte and bailey**: castle with a mound (motte) and a fenced yard (bailey). **Feudal system**: land given in return for loyalty and service. **Baron**: powerful Norman who held land directly from the king. **Domesday Book**: 1086 survey of England.

### 5. Timeline
```
Jan 1066 --- 25 Sep --- 28 Sep --- 14 Oct --- 25 Dec --- 1086
Edward dies  Stamford   Normans    Hastings   William    Domesday
Harold king  Bridge     land                  crowned    Book
```

### 6. Common mistakes
| Not this | But this |
|---|---|
| William fought Harold at Stamford Bridge | Harold fought **Hardrada** there; he fought William at **Hastings** |
| Harold was an easy winner at Hastings | Harold's army was **exhausted** after marching 250 miles |
| Castles were stone from the start | The first were **timber** motte and baileys |
| Domesday Book was a story about William | It was a **survey of land and wealth** for tax |
| Harold was already king by right | He was **chosen** by the Witan, and others also had claims |

### 7. Links
**Builds on:** Anglo-Saxon England and Viking raids. **Leads to:** the Normans' changes to church, language and law; medieval kings and the Magna Carta.

## PAGE 3: TEN RETRIEVAL QUESTIONS (TEACHER COPY WITH ANSWERS)

All are short-answer, so there is no multiple-choice answer key to pattern-check.

1. Name the four claimants to the throne in 1066. **Harold Godwinson, William of Normandy, Harald Hardrada, Edgar the Ætheling.**
2. What was William's reason for claiming the throne? **Edward promised it to him, and Harold swore an oath to support him in 1064.**
3. Who did Harold defeat at Stamford Bridge, and on what date? **Harald Hardrada (and Tostig), 25 September 1066.**
4. Where did William land, and how soon after Stamford Bridge? **Pevensey, three days later.**
5. Why was Harold at a disadvantage at Hastings? **His army was tired from marching about 250 miles after a battle.**
6. Give two reasons William won at Hastings. **Any two: archers, cavalry, feigned retreat, English broke the shield wall.**
7. On what date was Hastings fought? **14 October 1066.**
8. What is a motte and bailey castle, and what was it first made of? **A castle with a mound and a fenced yard; timber.**
9. Explain the feudal system in one sentence. **The king gave land to barons in return for loyalty and soldiers, who passed it down to knights and peasants.**
10. What was the Domesday Book, when was it made and why? **A survey of who owned what in England, 1086, so the king could tax fairly.**
