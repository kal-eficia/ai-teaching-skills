# Year 8 French homework: "Hier, j'ai..." (due next lesson)

**Time:** 25 minutes in total (Part A 8 min, Part B 12 min, Part C 5 min)
**AI:** No AI for this one. It is all retrieval from memory, so the learning is the effort of remembering. Using AI would skip exactly the bit that helps.
**Parent role:** Parents don't need any French (see Part C).

Assumptions: the class has met regular -er verbs in the perfect tense (j'ai mangé, elle a joué) and nothing else yet. I've kept all the French to -er verbs for that reason.

---

## Student sheet

### Part A: Quick-fire quiz (8 minutes, no notes)
Circle the correct answer. Do it from memory first.

**1.** Hier, j'___ mangé une pizza.
A) as  B) ai  C) a  D) avons

**2.** Elle ___ regardé un film.
A) ont  B) ai  C) avez  D) a

**3.** What is the past participle of *jouer*?
A) joué  B) jouer  C) joue  D) jouant

**4.** Nous ___ écouté de la musique.
A) a  B) ont  C) avons  D) ai

**5.** How do you say "They ate fish"?
A) Ils mange du poisson  B) Ils ont manger du poisson  C) Ils a mangé du poisson  D) Ils ont mangé du poisson

**6.** What does *ma tante* mean?
A) my uncle  B) my aunt  C) my cousin  D) my grandmother

**7.** What does *Je déteste le sport parce que c'est ennuyeux* mean?
A) I hate sport because it is boring  B) I love sport because it is boring  C) I hate sport because it is fun  D) I like sport because it is fun

**8.** How do you say "my brother"?
A) ma frère  B) mes frère  C) mon frère  D) mon frères

### Part B: Write about last weekend (12 minutes)
Write **5 sentences in French** about what you and your family did last weekend (real or made up). Use the frame below. You must use *avoir* + a past participle in every sentence.

> Le weekend dernier, **ma mère** a ___ (regarder / écouter / préparer).
> **Mon frère** a ___ (jouer / manger / regarder).
> **Nous** avons ___ .
> **J'**ai ___ .
> **J'**ai aimé / Je n'ai pas aimé ___ **parce que** c'est ___ .

Word bank: regardé, joué, écouté, mangé, préparé, travaillé, la télé, au foot, de la musique, une pizza, génial, drôle, ennuyeux. Use your own family words too (ma sœur, mes grands-parents, mon père...).

**Check yourself:** for each sentence, put a tick if (1) you used the right part of *avoir* for the person, and (2) your verb ends in **é**, with the accent.

**Stretch (optional):** write one more sentence with *j'ai fait* (I did/made) or *j'ai fini* (I finished), then say which family member it is about.

### Part C: Say it out loud (5 minutes)
Read your 5 sentences aloud to someone at home. They don't need to know any French. After each sentence, **they ask "What does that mean?" and you explain it in English.** If you can explain it, you know it. Ask them to sign here: ________

## Teacher answers and checking

**Part A key (1 mark each, 8 total):** 1 B, 2 D, 3 A, 4 C, 5 D, 6 B, 7 A, 8 C
Key is **BDACDBAC**, run through a pattern checker and clean (no repeats, runs, staircases, alternation or repeating blocks; each letter appears twice).
Spread: Q1-5 are the perfect tense with avoir; Q6 is family (last term); Q7 is opinions with parce que (last term); Q8 is family vocabulary (last term). Roughly 70% current content, 30% older.

**Part B marking guide (10 marks, 2 per sentence):**
- 1 mark: correct form of *avoir* for the subject (ai / a / avons / ont)
- 1 mark: correct past participle ending in é
- Do not penalise spelling elsewhere. Bonus (not counted): a correct opinion with *parce que*.

Model sentences:
- Le weekend dernier, ma mère a regardé la télé.
- Mon frère a joué au foot.
- Nous avons mangé une pizza.
- J'ai écouté de la musique.
- J'ai aimé le film parce que c'est drôle.

Common errors to watch for: *j'a / je ai* (elision missed), infinitive instead of participle (*a manger*), missing accent (*mange* vs *mangé*), *mon/ma* mix-ups, and using *a* with *nous* or *ils*.
Stretch model: J'ai fait mes devoirs. / Mon père a fini son travail.

**How it's checked:** Five minutes next lesson. Students swap for Part A and mark with the key on the board, then you collect Part B and mark just the two ticks per sentence. Part C is a signature only. Use the most common errors you see as the starter in the next lesson.
