# Year 8 French Homework: Talking About the Past with *avoir*

**Due:** next lesson | **Time:** about 30 minutes, ideally split over two evenings

**Assumptions I've made:** the class has met regular verbs (-er, -ir, -re) and a few common irregular participles (*fait, vu, pris, eu, bu*) in the perfect tense with *avoir*, and no verbs that take *être* yet. Parents get a jargon-free guide below and don't need any French.

---

## Part 1: Letter to parents (copy into the planner or send by email)

Dear parents and carers,

This week your child is practising how to say what they **did** in French (for example, "I watched a film" or "we played football"). You don't need to speak French to help. Here is how:

1. **Be the audience.** Ask your child to read their sentences aloud to you. Listening is a real help.
2. **Use the answer key** on the last page to check Parts 1 to 3 together. Marking is as simple as matching what's on the page.
3. **Ask the three questions** below and let them explain. Teaching you is one of the best ways to learn.
   - "What do you put in front of the action word?" (*j'ai, tu as, il/elle a, nous avons, vous avez, ils/elles ont*)
   - "What happens to the end of the action word?" (*-er* verbs end in *-é*)
   - "How do you say what you think about it?" (*j'aime, je déteste, parce que...*)
4. **Use free tools for pronunciation.** Type any French sentence into Google Translate or Google Lens and press the speaker icon to hear it. Your child can copy the sound.
5. **Please don't correct the French.** If you're unsure, leave it. I'll go through it in class. Just sign the sheet to show it was done.

If something took far longer than 30 minutes, please stop and write a note on the sheet. That's useful information for me, not a problem.

Thank you for your support!

## Part 2: The student homework

### Quick reminder (keep this beside you)

| Who | *avoir* | + past participle |
|---|---|---|
| je | **j'ai** | mangé |
| tu | **as** | regardé |
| il / elle | **a** | joué |
| nous | **avons** | fini |
| vous | **avez** | vendu |
| ils / elles | **ont** | fait |

**-er verbs:** drop *-er*, add **-é** (jouer → joué)
**-ir verbs:** drop *-ir*, add **-i** (finir → fini)
**-re verbs:** drop *-re*, add **-u** (vendre → vendu)
**Tricky ones to learn:** faire → **fait**, voir → **vu**, prendre → **pris**, avoir → **eu**, boire → **bu**

---

Write the correct part of *avoir* (**ai, as, a, avons, avez, ont**).

3. Tu _____ regardé un film.
4. Nous _____ fini les devoirs.
5. Mon frère _____ joué au foot.
6. Vous _____ acheté un cadeau.

### Task 3: Translate into French (5 marks)
Use your reminder box. Remember to say who did it, then *avoir*, then the participle.

1. I watched a film with my sister.
2. We played football with my cousins.
3. You (*tu*) finished your homework.
4. My grandmother bought a present. I love it because it is great.
5. My dad made a cake.

*Hints: ma soeur, mes cousins, mes devoirs, ma grand-mère, un cadeau, mon père, un gâteau, génial.*

### Task 4: Write about it (10 marks)
Write **6 to 8 sentences** in French about something you or your family did last weekend. You can invent it, as it doesn't have to be true!

You must include:
- [ ] **At least 4 sentences** in the perfect tense with *avoir* (use at least 3 different people: *j'ai, il a, nous avons, ils ont*...)
- [ ] **At least 2 family members** (*mon frère, ma mère, mes cousins, ma tante*...)
- [ ] **At least 2 opinions with *parce que*** (*J'aime ça parce que c'est amusant. Je déteste ça parce que c'est ennuyeux.*)

**Sentence starters if you're stuck:**
- Samedi, j'ai regardé...
- Ma mère a préparé...
- Mon frère et moi, nous avons joué...
- Mes cousins ont mangé...
- J'aime ça parce que c'est...
- Je déteste ça parce que c'est...

**Useful words:** amusant (fun), ennuyeux (boring), délicieux (delicious), génial (great), nul (rubbish), un film, un gâteau, un match, la télé

## Part 4: Notes for you, the teacher

- **Why it's built this way:** Tasks 1 to 3 are closed, so parents can check them with the key and no French. Task 4 recycles last half-term's family and opinion language so it stays fresh, and gives a more able child room to stretch.
- **Parent support without French:** the three questions in the letter let a parent take part by asking, not correcting.
- **Support option:** for pupils who struggle, let them use only *j'ai* and *il/elle a* and drop the target to 3 perfect-tense sentences.
- **Stretch option:** add *hier, samedi dernier, après* and a negative (*je n'ai pas aimé*) for the strongest pupils.
- **Starter for next lesson:** quick-fire "Who has *a*, who has *ont*?" mini-whiteboard round using Task 1 sentences, then 2 minutes of pairs reading Task 4 aloud.
